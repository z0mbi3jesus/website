How we start

A clear path from first conversation to approved work.

  1. Start with a free consultation. Tell us what you want to accomplish and what is getting in the way.
  2. Understand your business. We learn how you work, which tools you use, and what technology needs to do for you.
  3. Evaluate the options. We compare possible solutions and determine whether 6th Level is the right provider.
  4. Review a proposal. If we are a good fit, we outline the scope, pricing, and expectations so you can make an informed decision.
  5. Approve before work begins. We begin the proposed work after you approve the proposal.

Honest, knowledgeable advice comes first. If an existing solution or another provider would serve you better, we are willing to recommend it.
